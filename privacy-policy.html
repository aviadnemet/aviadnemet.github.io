Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to Android applications developed and published by Aviad Nemet, including the application "English for the Psychometric Exam" ("אנגלית לפסיכומטרי"). It explains what information the application collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Summary

  • The application does not require an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, or precise location.
  • Your study progress is stored on your device.
  • The application uses Google services (Firebase and AdMob) that automatically collect device identifiers, usage and diagnostic data.
  • Information you type into the contact form or a word report (including an optional reply email) is sent to the developer.

Information Stored on Your Device

Study progress, spaced-repetition data, favorite words, session history, settings, and other preferences are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded by the application. Uninstalling the application or clearing its data removes this information.

If you use the backup feature, the backup file is saved to a location you choose and is not uploaded by the application. Daily reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Information You Choose to Send

The application sends information to the developer only when you choose to:

  • Contact form: your message, the feedback type, an optional reply email address, the app version, the Android version, and the device model.
  • Word reports: the reported word, the report type, any text you add, and the app version.
  • Promo codes: when you redeem a code, the code, the time of redemption, and the app version, so that each code can only be used once per installation.

This information is stored in Google Cloud Firestore together with an anonymous identifier created by Firebase Authentication. It is used only to respond to you, to fix vocabulary errors, and to validate promo codes. Your email address is used only to reply to your message.

Information Collected Automatically

The application uses the following Google services, which collect some information automatically:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: app interactions (such as screens viewed, sessions, and features used), in-app purchase events, app-instance ID, advertising ID, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Used to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash logs and diagnostic information (such as device model, Android version, and app state at the time of a crash). Used to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config, Installations, and Authentication: a Firebase installation ID and an anonymous user ID. Used to deliver app settings and to secure the features described above. No personal account is created.
  • Google AdMob: advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate general location), app interactions, and diagnostic information. Used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. See "Advertisements" below.

The application also downloads a public vocabulary-corrections file from Firebase Hosting. This request does not include personal information beyond standard network information such as your IP address.

All data sent by the application and these services is encrypted in transit.

Sharing of Information

The developer does not sell your personal information. Data collected by Google AdMob (advertising ID, approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic data) is shared with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Firebase services process data on the developer's behalf as service providers.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review: https://policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Advertisements

The free version of the application displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID and usage information to display relevant ads and measure their performance.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). Premium users do not see ads, and the advertising SDK is not started for them.

In-App Purchases

If you purchase premium features within the application, payment transactions are processed securely by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store payment information such as credit card details. Purchase events (such as the product purchased) are recorded by Google Analytics for Firebase.

Data Retention and Deletion

Contact messages, word reports, and promo code redemptions are kept for as long as needed to handle them. Analytics and crash data are kept according to the retention settings of the Google services described above.

You can request deletion of messages or reports you sent by contacting the developer at the address below. Because the application does not use accounts, please include enough details (for example, the date and the email address you used) to locate them.

Children's Privacy

This application is not directed to children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
